Status badges

The presence mark on an avatar: online, idle, do not disturb, offline, online on mobile, streaming, and typing. Discord cuts the mark's ring out of the avatar with an SVG mask, so whatever sits behind shows through. Discord's geometry and colours below are its own numbers, read from Discord's client (2026-11-24). The Waffle columns show status_mark and Badged::status as they were before the port; Waffle now draws Discord's marks and their morph (src/ui/status_mark.rs).

Discord source: size table Kj (size / status / stroke / offset per avatar size); avatar cut-outs G (round), B (mobile), k (typing); mark shapes svg-mask-status-*; the animated mark v() and its colours S3 in module 935154.

Not verified: the morph's exact spring (Discord animates with react-spring; drawn here as a 320ms ease with a slight overshoot), and the typing dots' size and timing (their mask was read, not the dots inside it).

What broke (fixed)

In the DM list every mark draws as a plain filled disc: idle has no moon, do not disturb no bar, offline no hole. The shapes in status_mark haven't changed. They were moved unchanged from layout.rs into ui/face.rs in a4d59e8. Over the native sidebar the surface is clear, so cut_out uses backdrop_erase instead of painting the surface colour. The ring around the mark erases correctly. The cut-outs inside the mark don't show, so the mark's own fill seems to cover them.

Not verified: why the inner erase loses to its parent's fill (paint order of the erase primitive against the mark's background, or the mark's overflow_hidden mask). Check in the app before fixing. Discord's approach sidesteps it: the mark is its own shape (a masked fill), not a fill with holes punched in it.
