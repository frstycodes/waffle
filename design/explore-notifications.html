Notification Settings

Discord's server Notification Settings dialog, item for item, in Waffle's grouped-settings look. Opened from the server menu and the server dropdown. Every change saves at once, like Discord; Done only closes. Mute the server to see what it locks: the levels, Suppress Highlights (held on) and Mobile Push (held off), and every override.

2 · Clearing a mention from the Inbox

Hovering a mention shows Jump and Discord's checkmark, "Mark As Read". It removes the mention for good (on every device) without marking the channel read; the card leaves in place and the list closes the gap. Mentions are kept 7 days.

3 · Mark Unread

No new UI: the existing "Mark unread" item, ⌥ + click on a message and ⌥ ↩ on a focused one all move the read line to just above that message and now tell Discord (an ack of the message before it with manual: true and how many mentions sit below), so it stays unread on your other devices. The channel's unread pill and the "NEW" divider show as they do today. Two shortcuts join the Keybinds page: ⌘ I toggles the Inbox and ⌘ ⇧ E marks its top unread channel read.
