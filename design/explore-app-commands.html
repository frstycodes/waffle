App commands

Slash commands, what an app's reply looks like, the buttons and menus apps attach, Components V2 layouts and app forms. Discord's behaviour, Waffle's look: chips and dialogs from the primitives we already ship. Do better than Discord: only commands you can run here, your most used first, failures say which app and offer a retry.

1 · Typing / opens the picker

At the start of the composer. Apps with commands here sit in a rail (Recent first); the list groups by app and filters as you type, matching the name and the description. ↑↓ move, Tab or Enter picks, Esc closes. Commands you lack permission for aren't listed.

2 · Filling in options

The picked command becomes a chip; each required option follows as a key/value chip, the active one ringed. Above the composer: what the active option wants, and the optional ones to add. Choices and autocomplete list underneath it; a missing required option stops Enter and names itself.

4 · Buttons and select menus

Up to five buttons a row, in the app's styles mapped to ours: primary is our accent, success and danger keep their meaning, link opens the browser and says so. A select opens our menu; multi-select uses checks and a Done. A clicked button shows its own spinner until the app answers; disabled ones dim.

6 · App forms (modals)

When a button or command asks for a form, the app's fields open in our dialog: its title, which app is asking, text fields (short or long) with required marks and limits, Cancel and Submit. Submitting shows the spinner in Submit; an error from the app shows under the fields and keeps what you typed.
