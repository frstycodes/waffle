Better than Discord

Waffle copies Discord's features, not its weak spots. These 165 changes are gathered from every page; each page's own "Better than Discord" section shows them beside their demos. Discord's side is described from memory, not re-checked against the current client.

Flows

3 changes. See them with their demos

  • Over-limit message: Discord blocks with a modal Waffle turns it into a .txt attachment in one click.
  • Delete own message: Discord confirms Waffle deletes at once with Undo (Shift skips toast).
  • Offline sends: queue in order and send on reconnect.

Context menus

6 changes. See them with their demos

  • Mute lengths show when they end; muted row reads "Muted until 9:41 PM".
  • Disabled rows say why on a second line.
  • Moving: one submenu marking the current spot; servers can go after any server.
  • Delete own message: instant with 6s Undo; others' still confirm with the message shown.
  • Confirm buttons name outcome and target ("Stay" / "Leave Night Shift").
  • Timeout: dialog showing each length's end time plus an audit-log reason.

Sign in and states

14 changes. See them with their demos

  • Splash names the real stage, never longer than 25s; no tips or endless spinner.
  • QR says where the scanner is (Settings, then Scan QR Code).
  • Failed sign-in: plain explanation, Discord's reason verbatim, Try again.
  • 2FA: six boxes, paste works, checks on the last digit, no submit button.
  • Token sign-in behind a disclosure with the warning before the field.
  • Account switcher from your panel with status and mention counts; log out instant with Undo.
  • Channel welcome header names the channel once, then topic and creator.
  • Connection lost: chat stays readable, what you write queues.
  • Offline at launch: opens Waffle by itself when the network returns.
  • Rate limit: countdown on the composer, text kept.
  • No permission: names the role that can read the channel.
  • Outage explained in the main view.
  • Updates download in background and wait for a call to end.
  • Failed edit/delete toasts say what happened and offer the fix.
