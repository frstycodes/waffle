Discord vs Waffle

Discord on the left, Waffle's prototype on the right (live from explore-navigation.html, so it follows the theme switch). Discord's side is a recreation, not a screenshot: labels, order, limits and rules are from its client code (docs/discord-navigation-re.md, docs/discord-moderation-re.md, docs/discord-menus-*.md); colours from its midnight theme tokens; spacing, icons and exact layout from memory, so treat those as approximate. The list under each pair is what differs.

Pinned DMs

  • Menu: Discord pairs Pin with Add to favorites in one group; Waffle now does too. Discord's Pin icon trails the label (its menus put icons at the end); Waffle's lead, as all Waffle menus do.
  • Row: same rule (pin at the end, no divider, pinned first by recency). Discord keeps the pin beside a mention badge; Waffle does too.

Pinned channels

  • Menu order: Discord puts Add to favorites alone right under Mark as read, and Pin channel to top in the Invite / Remove from channel list / Copy link group. Waffle now uses the same groups.
  • Section header: Discord's "Pinned Channels" has no collapse caret; Waffle's matches. Discord's has no hover "+" either; same.
  • Dialog: same copy and buttons.

Favorites

  • Origin server: Waffle shows each favourite's server name at the row's end; Discord shows none in the row (from memory). Keep it: it's Waffle's look, not a behaviour change, and favourites from two servers can share a name.
  • Move to: Discord's favourite menu has a Move to submenu (into one of your categories); Waffle now has it.
  • Header menu: Discord's Favorites dropdown holds Thread settings and Hide favorites; Waffle now has Create category and Hide favorites; Thread settings is left out (it auto-adds threads, which Waffle won't do).
  • Rail: same place (after Home, before the DM bubbles) and the same summed mention badge.

Show all channels

  • Grouping: Discord groups Show all channels with Notification settings and Privacy settings, and Hide muted channels with Edit per-server profile. Waffle keeps its own shorter menu; the new check goes in Waffle's notification group, matching Discord's neighbour.
  • Check style: Discord's checks are filled boxes at the end; Waffle uses its own check primitive. Look, not behaviour.

Role mention popout

  • Header: Discord shows the role's icon only when it has one, with no colour dot; Waffle now matches.
  • Rows: Discord's rows are its member-list rows, so they keep the owner crown and boost mark; Waffle now reuses its member-list row.

Mod View

  • Close: Discord's close X sits by the member's name, and the title bar shows an esc keycap; Waffle puts close in the panel header like its other panels and shows no keycap. Look, not behaviour: Esc works the same.
  • Sections: Discord boxes each section on a raised card; Waffle separates them by headings only, as its panels do.
  • Same: section order, labels, the action row and its per-button gates, Esc closing / stepping back.

Members: signals

  • Columns: Discord also has Join method (from members/supplemental) and Roles; Waffle now has both.
  • Signals filter: Discord puts it in the Signals column header's menu, not a toolbar chip. Waffle's toolbar chip is a look choice; the filter itself is the same OR'd set.
  • Paging: Discord pages 12 / 25 / 50 / 100 and titles the list "Recent members" until you search. Waffle now has both.
  • Checkbox column: Discord's is for bulk actions behind an experiment; Waffle dropped them, since it has no bulk actions.
