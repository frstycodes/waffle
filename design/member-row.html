Member row

The person row: avatar with presence, name with its decorators, one line of what they're doing. Discord draws it in the member list, the DM list, a group DM's member list, under voice channels and on the Friends page. Discord's behaviour is read from its shipped client (discord.com bundle and CSS, 2026-11-24); Waffle's is member_row (src/panel.rs), dm_row (src/sidebar/dm_row.rs), voice_rows (src/sidebar/voice.rs) and friend_row (src/home.rs) on main. Both columns follow the page theme.
