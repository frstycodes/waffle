Group DMs, Active Now, Message Requests

Behaviour, copy, limits and endpoints follow Discord's client (docs/discord-*-re.md). Surfaces are Waffle's: the DM list, friends page, dialogs and menus already in the app. Proposed "do better" touches are marked Better.

1. Group DMs

A group gets Discord's header tools: call, video, pins, Add Members (the person-plus icon) and the member list toggle. The member list is the floating member card, owner marked with a crown. Right-click a member for Discord's group items; right-click the group in the DM list for Edit Group and Leave Group.

The member menu shows Remove From Group and Make Group Owner only to the owner, and never on the owner's own row. Remove is instant, as in Discord: no confirm.

The picker: New Group DM, Add to DM, Invite to Group DM

One dialog, three entry points: the Friends header's New Group DM icon, Add Members in a 1:1 DM (which makes a new group and leaves the DM alone), and Add Members or Invite to Group DM in a group. Friends only; 10 including you. Better: the limit is a meter, ten segments, yours filled grey, your picks in the accent, "6 of 10".

Edit Group, Leave Group, Transfer Ownership

Edit Group: name up to 100 characters (the auto name is the placeholder), icon up to 8 MB, Remove Icon. Save stays dim until something changes. Leave Group: Discord's confirm, with its "leave without notifying" checkbox. Better: when the owner leaves, the confirm names who takes over.

2. Friends: Active Now

Add Friend already ships. Active Now is Discord's right column: one card per friend or party, grouped by voice channel or party, sorted voice and streams first, Spotify last. Hidden under 1200px window width. Click a card for the profile; Join on voice and streams. Errors on Add Friend now use Discord's copy per code (below).

3. Message Requests and Spam

Message Requests joins Friends at the top of the DM list only while a request or spam is waiting; its badge counts requests, not spam. Request and spam DMs leave the normal DM list. Click a row to read it in a side panel before deciding. Better: rows show mutual servers, and Spam rows get Not spam beside Report Spam.
