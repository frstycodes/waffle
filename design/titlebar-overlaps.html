Title bar overlaps

Every place Waffle draws on the window's top line, where Windows' caption buttons (top right, 138×48) and macOS's traffic lights (top left, in the 36px strip) sit. Each header that reaches a corner leaves room for it. Two lint rules hold it (src/ui/rules.tsv): titlebar-clearance blocks a title-line drag area whose function never reserves that room, and float-edges blocks a floating surface kept only 8px off the window's top.
