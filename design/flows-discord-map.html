Stream errors

Discord's error codes for streams, from its error table and the checks that raise them.

CodeDiscordWaffle
2001 stream failed to startWatch not connected in time, or unauthorized: failed screen with the code.Matches as under Watch fails to connect.
2002 low frame rate (viewing)The watched stream decodes under 3 / 8 / 15 / 30 fps for streams of up to 5 / 15 / 30 / 60 fps, over a short or long window.Differs the Choppy chip (under 15 fps) carries the code; Waffle doesn't know a remote stream's own frame rate. Fixed a cause: on a still screen Waffle sent 2 frames a second, which Discord viewers flag as 2002. It now repeats the frame at the stream's full rate.
2003 packet loss (viewing)Over 10% lost on a watched stream.Matches measured per 5 s after retransmissions; the stream's health label reads Packet loss, with the code in its tooltip.
2004 / 2005 / 2006 (streaming)Your stream: over 10% loss (2004) or encoding under the frame-rate line (2005), both only while someone watches; the stream connection rated bad, past 500 ms ping or 10% loss (2006).Matches a warning mark beside LIVE on your stream's tile lists each with its code, gated the same way.
2007 reconnectingA dropped stream reconnects, with the code on the tile.Matches reconnecting overlay with the code.
2008 / 2009 decode, encode errorReported to Discord's analytics only; nothing is shown.Matches nothing shown; a decode error asks for a keyframe, and an encode error falls back to OpenH264.
2010 stream fullCloses the stream and says it's full, once per stream (10 s before it may say so again).Differs closes it and says "{name}'s stream is full" every time.
2011–2015 ready timeoutsYour camera or stream sends no picture (2011, 2014), or theirs never arrives (2012, 2015); camera under 10 fps while others could see it (2013).Differs 2011, 2012 and 2013 are raised: a stream with no picture shows No video yet with Try again, and a camera gets a warning mark. Waffle has no separate case for 2014/2015 (video never announced). Not verified: Discord's timeout; Waffle waits 10 s.
1001 / 3004 sound share, OS capture errorYour stream's audio or screen capture failed.Matches 1001 when your stream's sound stops, as a warning mark on your stream; 3004 when the capture fails to start or the system stops it, said with its code.
