Upload and send options

Parity gaps 1.18 and 1.16. Behaviour, limits and copy are Discord's, from docs/discord-upload-alt-spoiler-re.md and docs/discord-send-options-re.md; the look is Waffle's. Everything here is clickable.

1. Files waiting to send

Three directions; pick one. Each tile keeps Discord's three buttons in its order: Spoiler Attachment (eye), Modify Attachment (pencil), Remove Attachment, shown on hover and keyboard focus. Alt and Spoiler tags sit on the picture; spoiler pictures stay veiled.

Message #photos

2. Modify Attachment

Filename, then Description (Alt Text) (images and videos only, 1,024 characters, placeholder "Add a description"), then Mark as spoiler. A filename starting SPOILER_ ticks and locks the box. Enter in the filename saves. After sending, the same dialog opens with the filename locked.

Modify Attachment

3. Sent images

Your own message: hovering an attachment shows Modify Attachment beside Delete; only the author gets it. Descriptions show only with Show image descriptions on (off by default, see 6): an ALT button on each tile of a mosaic, opening Image Description (Alt Text); a single image gets a muted caption instead.

4. @everyone and @here

Enter on a message that pings @everyone or @here (outside code) in a channel of more than 30 people, when you can ping them, opens Hold Up! over the composer instead of sending. The count is cut to its leading digit with a "+"; @here leaves out offline members. Esc goes back to editing, Enter sends. Role mentions never ask. Threads and forum posts say "in this thread" / "in this post".

Hold Up!

5. @silent and /tts

Starting a message with @silent styles the prefix as a token; it is stripped before sending and the message goes out with no push or desktop notification. Everyone sees a sleeping bell beside the name: "This is a @silent message."

Typing /tts offers Discord's built-in command. Servers only, with Send Text-to-Speech Messages and the setting on. People with the channel open hear "Mira said …"; the message itself looks normal.
