Pins and Favorites

Parity gaps 2.1, 2.2 and 2.16, built to Discord's behaviour (docs/discord-navigation-re.md, docs/discord-moderation-re.md) in Waffle's look. Back and Forward already ship as keys only (Cmd+[ and ], Alt+arrows elsewhere, mouse buttons 4 and 5); the one change there is that Friends joins the history, as Discord's /channels/@me route does. No buttons.

Pinned DMs

Right-click a DM or group DM: Pin / Unpin. Pinned conversations sit first, both groups newest message first, no manual order. No header or divider, only a pin at the row's end (it gives way to the close button on hover). Synced through the DM's override flag, so it carries across devices. No limit.

Pinned channels

Channel menu: Pin channel to top / Unpin channel (not for threads or categories). Pinned channels leave their category and gather under Pinned channels, above the uncategorised ones, in server order with voice after text. They always show, whatever the mute, collapse or Show all channels state. Pins are only yours. Try pinning and unpinning below.

Dragging a pinned channel

Pinned rows don't drag and nothing drops onto them. Trying it asks:

Trying to reorder a pinned channel?

Unpin this channel before reordering. Pinning is only for you, but channel order applies to everyone.

Favorites

A star tile after Home in the rail, shown once you've favourited something. Its badge sums mentions across its channels. Channel menu: Add to favorites / Remove from favorites. Inside Favorites, rows carry a nickname you set (Change nickname), can sit in your own categories (Create category, Move to, Rename category), and offer Go to server. The Favorites header menu holds Create category and Hide favorites. Synced through settings-proto field 15. The limit is 3 channels without Nitro and 200 with it.

Favorites will show up here!Right-click any channel and pick Add to favorites.

Over the limit

Oh no!

You cannot have more than 3 favorite channels. Get Nitro to favorite up to 200.

Show all channels

A check in the server menu of Community servers. Unticked, the list keeps only channels you've added (and anything selected, mentioning you or with active threads, plus pins). Channel menu then offers Add to channel list / Remove from channel list. Unticking it the first time adds every text and voice channel to your list, so nothing vanishes at once.

Role mention popout

Clicking an @role opens a card to its right: the role's icon (only when it has one) and name, its member count, then the members you have loaded with that role, by display name; "+N more" when the count runs past them. @everyone lists everyone loaded, without a count. @here and unknown roles open nothing. Rows are member-list rows (owner crown, boost mark) that open the profile on click and the member menu on right-click.

Mod View

For anyone who can moderate (Administrator, Manage server, Kick, Ban, Time out, Manage roles or Manage nicknames). Opened from the member menu's Open in Mod View or the profile card; it takes the right panel's place. Esc closes it, or steps back from a sub-page. Buttons you can't use are dimmed, not hidden.

Members: signals

Discord has no "new account" flag; account age is the Joined Discord column.
