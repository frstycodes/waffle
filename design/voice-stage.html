Voice stage

The call view that fills the main area while you're in, or looking at, a voice channel or call. Discord's behaviour is read from its shipped web client (discord.com bundle, lazy chunks and CSS, 2026-11-24); Waffle's is src/call.rs, src/call/*, src/stage_view.rs, src/pip.rs and src/dm_call.rs on main. Both stages are dark whatever the page theme (Discord forces its Onyx theme on a black call area; Waffle reads Graphite's dark tokens); the chat beside them follows the theme.
