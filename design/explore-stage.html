Hover the stage's hand button for requests; press the hand in the bar to raise it and press again to cancel; the chevron beside Leave holds End Stage for Everyone. The sidebar lists speakers under the stage and folds the audience into one row, as Discord does; the topic is the channel's second line. Moderators carry the shield on their name chip. No join or leave sounds on a stage.
