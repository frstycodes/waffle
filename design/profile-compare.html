Profile parity

Every place Discord shows a person's profile: the popout from a name or avatar, the DM's side panel, and the full profile dialog. Discord's side is drawn from its shipped client (CSS, layout specs and English strings, read 2026-11-24); Waffle's mirrors src/profile.rs, src/profile/{parts,full,dm_panel}.rs. Waffle keeps its identity: squircle corners, honey, its own icons and springs. Layout, order, copy and behaviour follow Discord.

Discord measures: popout 300 wide, banner 105, avatar 80 at 10/4, status bubble at 105px from the left and 7px above the banner's foot; DM side panel 300 wide, banner 105; full profile 962 × min(800, window − 96), its card min(max(300, 45.5vw − 43.68), 400), banner 140, avatar 120, padding and gutter 32. Section headings 12px semibold in default ink. Display name 20px semibold; username row 14px with pronouns, the guild tag pill and the badges trailing it.

Not verified: the badge icon size inside the username row (drawn at 20); the exact status-bubble fill on a themed card; whether the popout banner without a picture keeps all 105px for users without Nitro (the popout spec sets 105 regardless); colours of themed cards beyond the gradient math, which needs a live render.
