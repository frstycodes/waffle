Audio attachments

Any .mp3 .m4a .ogg .opus .wav .flac file that isn't a voice message. There's no speed control. When it ends, the play button becomes Play again. Focus the card for keys: Space plays or pauses, ←/→ jump 5 s, 0–9 jump to 0–90%.

Behaviour carried over from Discord

  • Speed cycles .75X, 1X, 1.5X, 2X. One speed for every voice message, remembered on this device. Pitch stays the same.
  • One volume for voice, audio and video, set on a curve that matches how loud it sounds. The speaker button mutes; hover it for the slider (↑/↓ 5%).
  • The waveform has 2 px bars on a 6 px pitch and 32 px tall. It is 40 px wide at 0.5 s or less, growing to 294 px at 45 s. Press on it to seek, drag to scrub (paused while dragging). No keys on the waveform itself.
  • Where you stopped is kept for the last 25 voice messages, if you stopped between 0.5 s and 95%. At the end it rewinds, pauses, and after 500 ms shows the total again.
  • Starting a voice message pauses any other voice message. Audio attachments and videos keep playing.
  • Message menu: no Edit Message and no Copy Text; Save File downloads the recording.
