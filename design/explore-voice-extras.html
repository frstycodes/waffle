Voice chat, status and soundboard

Parity §4.4, §4.5 and §4.2: behaviour and copy follow Discord (see docs/discord-*-re.md); the look is Waffle's. The call stage stays dark in both themes.

1. Voice channel rows

The status is the row's second line, inside its fill and hit area. Open Chat leads Invite and Edit on hover, and stays shown while you're in the channel. Unread chat only marks the channel you're in; mentions show the red count everywhere, and clicking a row with mentions joins and opens the chat.

3. In the call

The header carries the status under the channel name. New messages float in at the top right, three at most; they share one 10-second timer that restarts with each message and freeze while hovered. Clicking one opens the chat at that message. Hidden while the chat is open. The chat button shows mentions, else unread, as a count (99+).

4. Soundboard

Opened from its own control in the call bar (and ⌘⇧B). Click plays to everyone; Preview plays it only to you; the star favourites it (250 at most). The rail jumps between Favorites, Frequently Used, each server and Discord Sounds; other servers' sounds sit below a divider, locked without Nitro.

States

Playing: an outline and a bobbing emoji while it plays. Locked: another server's sound without Nitro, dimmed with Discord's reason in the tooltip. The control's tooltip is "Open Soundboard", or says why it's off: "Soundboard disabled when deafened", "…when server muted", "…when suppressed". No permission (Use Soundboard and Speak): the control is hidden. Discord has no cooldown UI; a rate-limited play fails silently.

The speaker in the search field opens "Soundboard Volume", the same value as Settings › Voice & Video. At 0 the icon crosses out and Preview says "Increase your Soundboard volume setting to preview". You hear others' sounds unless you're deafened or muted their soundboard; self-mute doesn't block them.
